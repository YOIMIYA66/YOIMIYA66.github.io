MED_AI智能医疗视觉Medical Vision“明眸八疾”双眼眼底智能辅助诊断系统Ophthalmic Disease Diagnosis

立足双眼视网膜生理对称性与早期单侧病变临床机理：青光眼、糖尿病视网膜病变（DR）早期常率先表现为单侧微血管不对称损伤，传统单眼模型极易漏诊。创新设计双眼孪生权重共享主干网络（Weight-shared Backbone）与跨眼特征差分交叉融合拓扑，精准建模不对称病理差分，解耦联合诊断 8 类眼疾，综合 AUC 达 98.4%。

Leveraging the physiological symmetry of bilateral retinas and early unilateral microvascular lesions in diseases like glaucoma and diabetic retinopathy (DR), designed bilateral weight-shared twin backbones with cross-eye differential feature fusion. Captures subtle asymmetric pathology missed by monocular models, achieving 98.4% multi-label diagnostic AUC across 8 retinal diseases.
